### MPL-2.0 clauses, accurately mapped

Retrieved primary text from Eigen's `COPYING.MPL2`:

- **§3.1 — Distribution of Source Form:** covered Source Code Form, including modifications, must be distributed under MPL; inform recipients it is MPL-governed and how to obtain the licence; do not restrict source-form rights.
- **§3.2(a) — Distribution of Executable Form:** also make covered code available in Source Code Form as §3.1 describes, and tell executable recipients how to obtain it “by reasonable means in a timely manner,” at no more than distribution cost.
- **§3.2(b):** the Executable Form may be MPL-licensed or sublicensed under different terms, **provided** those terms do not limit/alter recipients' rights in the Source Code Form.
- **§3.3 — Distribution of a Larger Work:** chosen terms are allowed for the Larger Work provided covered-code MPL requirements are met. This permits different terms for the project's own code; it is not a ban on commercial use or sale.
- **§3.4 — Notices:** do not remove/alter the substance of source-form licence/copyright/patent/warranty/liability notices except to remedy known factual inaccuracies.

The notices give the precise unmodified-source archive URL, version and digest, explaining how recipients obtain Eigen's source. That URL was retrieved successfully during this audit. Availability must remain reasonable/timely for §3.2(a); a project-controlled mirror is optional operational hardening, **not a newly invented licence clause**. No legal guarantee about a third-party URL's indefinite future availability is asserted.

A verified archive hash establishes downloaded bytes, not consumed headers by itself. `cmake/provision.cmake` has no patch/overlay step; later `release-audit/consumed-header-verification.json` addresses the consumed tree for its recorded Linux build. Do not generalise that evidence to other platforms or arbitrary caches.

## 4. Compiler/system runtimes

- **GCC Runtime Library Exception v3.1**, [primary text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gcc-exception-3.1.html), also retrieved from [GCC COPYING.RUNTIME](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/master/COPYING.RUNTIME). §1 permits eligible Target Code combinations with Independent Modules under chosen terms consistent with those modules' licences. It has **no** previously claimed §§4/5 replacement-runtime or mandatory-notice condition. COPYING.RUNTIME is 3,324 bytes; those earlier clauses/version claims were incorrect and are withdrawn.
- Recorded **actual Linux extension** dynamic dependencies (`final-direct-needed.log`) are libstdc++, libgcc_s, libc, libm and the loader. These are observations of that artefact, not an assumption that a build tool is a runtime component.
- **glibc:** LGPL-2.1-or-later. Not bundling a copy avoids redistributing that library binary, but dynamic linking is not a blanket exemption from the licence's notice and combined-work conditions. A suitable shared-library mechanism is one of LGPL §6's alternatives; do not import those conditions into the GCC exception. No legal opinion on the final combined-work notice set is made here. [LGPL-2.1 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html).
- **macOS/Windows:** libc++/LLVM-exception and Microsoft redistributable terms depend on actual linkage/bundling. These were not independently observed in final platform artefacts by this researcher. Do not assert that every platform bundles nothing based on Linux evidence alone.
